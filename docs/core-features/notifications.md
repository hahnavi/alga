# Notifications

Alga tells you about escalations, on-call handoffs, incidents, and mentions — where and how you want to hear about them.

## How you get notified

You choose this under **Settings → Notifications**. For each kind of update, pick where it should go:

- **Bell** — the notification bell at the top of Alga
- **Email**
- **Slack DM**
- **Phone call**

Click the bell to see recent notifications. Click any notification to jump straight to that alert or incident. Click "mark all as read" to clear the list.

## Looping someone in

Type `@name` in a comment to loop someone into the conversation. They get a notification through their own settings. Typing `@agent` brings the AI helper into the thread instead.

## On-call handoffs

When a shift changes, the person starting gets a "your shift started" note and the person leaving gets a "your shift ended" note with a nudge to leave handoff notes.

## Daily recap

Every morning Alga posts an AI-written recap of the last 24 hours — how many alerts came in, what incidents happened, and any trends. You will see it on the dashboard.

## Tips

- If notifications feel noisy, turn off channels per type in Settings → Notifications instead of ignoring everything.
- Make sure your phone number is set if you want voice calls, and check you haven't opted out of calls.
- Use Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) anywhere to quickly find an alert, incident, or investigation.
